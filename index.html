Jobs

Jobs on this phone
Set end gaps: you choose the distance from each end of the truss to the first and last fixture, and the rest are spaced evenly between. Equal gaps: the space at each end matches the gap between fixtures. Centred: each fixture sits in the middle of an equal share of truss, so the ends get half a gap. Fixed gap: you set the gap and the group is centred.
Turn on In-section distances to also get each mark measured from the end of the section it lands on (the end nearest where you measure from), so you can tape a single section on the deck. Fixtures close to a join get a flag, since clamps can foul the couplers.
A job holds all the trusses for one show. Jobs are saved on this phone; use Jobs ▾ to switch, or save a job file to send to crew or another phone.
